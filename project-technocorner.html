TECHNOCORNER UGM 2025

Remote-Controlled Robot Competition - Top 8 Finalist

🏅 Top 8 Robotics Remote Control 2025

Project Overview

TECHNOCORNER is one of the most prestigious technology competitions organized by Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM). The 2025 edition featured various categories, with our team participating in the Remote-Controlled Robot Competition.

Competing against over 80 teams from top universities nationwide, we successfully reached the top 8, demonstrating exceptional engineering skills, strategic planning, and precise robot control under high-pressure competition conditions.

Competition Format

The competition challenged teams to design and operate a remote-controlled robot capable of:

  • Navigating through complex terrain with obstacles
  • Picking up and transporting objects to designated zones
  • Climbing inclined surfaces and stairs
  • Operating with precision within strict time constraints
  • Maintaining stability and control throughout the course

Robot Design & Features

Our robot was engineered with advanced capabilities:

Mechanical Systems:

  • Four-wheel drive system with independent suspension
  • Custom-designed gripper mechanism for object manipulation
  • Reinforced aluminum chassis for durability
  • High-torque DC motors with gear reduction

Electronics & Control:

  • Arduino Mega 2560 as main controller
  • 2.4GHz wireless communication system
  • Custom PCB design for efficient wiring
  • Real-time camera feed for operator guidance
  • Servo motors for precise gripper control

Power System:

  • 12V LiPo battery pack with 5000mAh capacity
  • Voltage regulation circuits for stable operation
  • Battery monitoring system with low-voltage alert

Competition Strategy

Success in TECHNOCORNER required more than just a well-built robot:

  • Pre-competition Testing: Over 100 hours of practice runs to perfect control
  • Operator Training: Dedicated pilot training for smooth operation
  • Backup Systems: Redundant components to handle failures
  • Time Management: Optimized path planning to maximize points
  • Adaptability: Quick adjustments based on course conditions

Results & Achievements

Competition Performance:

  • 🏅 Reached Top 8 out of 80+ teams

  • Achieved highest stability score in our bracket
  • Received technical excellence recognition

Learning Outcomes:

Technical Challenges Overcome

Throughout the project, we encountered and solved several challenges:

  • Signal Interference: Implemented frequency hopping to avoid wireless conflicts
  • Weight Distribution: Redesigned chassis for optimal balance
  • Power Management: Optimized motor control to extend battery life
  • Gripper Precision: Fine-tuned servo calibration for accurate object handling
  • Terrain Adaptation: Modified suspension system for various surfaces
