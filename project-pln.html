PLN SustainAction 2025

Innovation & Sustainability Project - 🥉 3rd Place Winner

🏆 3rd Place Innovation Sustainability 2025

Project Overview

PLN SustainAction 2025 is a national innovation competition organized by PLN (Perusahaan Listrik Negara) focusing on sustainable energy solutions and environmental conservation through technology.

Our project, Lyvotem, proudly earned 3rd Place among hundreds of competing teams from universities across Indonesia. This achievement recognizes our innovative approach to addressing energy sustainability challenges.

Problem Statement

Indonesia faces significant challenges in:

  • Increasing energy demand with limited renewable resources
  • High electricity costs in remote areas
  • Environmental impact from traditional energy sources
  • Inefficient energy consumption in households and industries

Lyvotem: Our Innovation

Lyvotem is an intelligent IoT-based energy monitoring and optimization system that helps reduce electricity consumption and promotes sustainable energy usage.

Key Features:

  • Real-time Monitoring: Track energy consumption patterns through mobile app
  • Smart Analytics: AI-powered recommendations for energy optimization
  • Automated Control: Intelligent device scheduling to minimize waste
  • Cost Savings: Reduce electricity bills by up to 30%
  • Carbon Footprint Tracking: Monitor and reduce environmental impact

Technology Stack

  • Hardware: ESP32 microcontroller, Current sensors, Relay modules
  • Software: Python for backend, React Native for mobile app
  • Cloud: Firebase for real-time database and authentication
  • AI/ML: TensorFlow for energy consumption prediction
  • Communication: MQTT protocol for IoT device communication

Impact & Results

Competition Achievement:

  • 🥉 3rd Place Winner - PLN SustainAction 2025
  • Cash prize and mentoring opportunity
  • Recognition from PLN for innovation excellence

Project Impact:

  • Tested with 50+ households showing average 25% energy savings
  • Reduced carbon emissions equivalent to planting 100+ trees annually
  • Featured in university innovation showcase
  • Potential for commercialization and wider implementation
